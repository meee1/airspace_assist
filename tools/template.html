              Imports the route (rtept) from a GPX file, e.g. from OzRunways or AvPlan; if there's no route it uses the waypoints (wpt), then a track (trkpt). Points whose names match an aerodrome, VFR waypoint or navaid are labelled from the AIP. The next waypoint advances automatically within 0.5 NM or once you pass it, and drives Go to. The profile shows SRTM terrain and DAH airspace along the straight legs: tap it for details. It is for awareness, not obstacle clearance. In simulation, Fly follows the route. Save GPX downloads the plan as it is now, edits included. Edit (or New plan) adds waypoints by name or code with the same search as Go to (at the end, where they fit best, or as the next waypoint); ▲ ▼ move them, ✕ deletes and ⇄ replaces one with the next point you search for or pick from the map; press and hold the map (right-click on a computer) to add a point to the plan, or to start one.

              Create a free ArcGIS Location Platform account at developers.arcgis.com and make an API key with the basemap privileges. Best: put it in your Cloudflare Worker as the secret ESRI_KEY (the proxy address is set under NOTAMs and traffic); tiles then come through it, cached by Cloudflare and stored on this device for offline use as you view them. Without a proxy, enter the key here (restrict it to meee1.github.io); tiles are then only kept by the browser for a while. The Sat button under the map turns it on and off.

              NAIPS area page ↗
              Opens NAIPS in your browser, signed in as you (NOTAMs only, 12 h). Then Share the result to this app, copy and paste it, or save it as a PDF and open it here.
              Download with your NAIPS login
              Stored encrypted on this device only. Your proxy signs in to NAIPS, gets the briefing and keeps nothing. Unofficial: if it stops working, use the button above.
              Traffic (map button) is ADS-B from adsb.lol (adsb.fi as a fallback) via the traffic proxy, refreshed every 10 s with signal. It shows only aircraft transmitting ADS-B that a ground receiver hears, typically 5–10 s late and patchy at low level. It is not TCAS and not a collision-avoidance system: keep looking out.
              NOTAMs come only from the NAIPS briefing you load here, kept for use offline. Get a fresh one before each flight.
