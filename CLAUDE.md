# CLAUDE.md — airspace_assist (Radio Calls AU)

Phone and tablet web app for Australian VFR pilots, especially helicopters. Target phone: Google Pixel 9 Pro (Chrome on Android); iPhones work too; 8–10" Android tablets get a two-column (portrait) or fixed-map (landscape) layout. It shows:
- the closest aerodrome (or the next one on the flight plan), with CTAF, ATIS/AWIS, tower and approach frequencies
- the aerodrome's runways (list plus a small to-scale diagram) and ERSA right-hand circuits
- a radio call guide that switches between CTAF and Class D procedures, with a runway picker and ERSA reporting points (split towers follow your side and ERSA's reporting-point rules, e.g. Moorabbin via CARR on 123.0)
- status tiles: GPS, TRK/HDG (compass auto-aligned to GPS track), PLAN (bearing and turn to the active waypoint), GS, ALT/AGL, CLASS (class here, cap, LL), and a second row with the CTAF of the closest aerodrome and the AREA (or ATC) frequency at your position, each with the expected next frequency and its distance; a row of the closest controlled aerodrome's ATIS/TWR/GND/APP; dashed Go to tiles with the CTAF/TWR and area/ATC frequency where you're heading; a red NEXT CLASS row when class A/C/D or a restricted area starts within 10 NM ahead at your altitude
- the area frequency and the airspace stack for your position and altitude
- an airspace map at the top of the page (pan, pinch zoom, track/north up) with a 10 NM lead line, SRTM terrain, a CTAF layer, a VTC/VNC chart underlay, runways, VFR waypoints, navaids, towns and suburbs (CTAF, Terrain, Charts, Lead and Log toggle; Traffic button), with NOTAM circles and ADS-B traffic
- Go to with fuzzy search, phonetic-alphabet decoding, a mic button and a press-and-hold map menu (place, lat/lon, and ERSA SUA details for any R/D areas there), and GPX flight plans (or plans built on the phone: add by search or from the map, reorder, replace, delete) with auto-sequencing, direct-to with Resume plan, Save GPX, an elevation profile, suggested VFR altitudes and a flight log
- NOTAMs from a NAIPS briefing only (paste/share/PDF; the FAA NMS proxy feed was removed 29 Oct 2026) and ADS-B traffic (adsb.lol, adsb.fi fallback, through the owner's Cloudflare Worker proxy, TCAS-style display and alerts)
- GPS altitude converted from the WGS84 ellipsoid to MSL with an EGM96 grid
- all of it offline once loaded (live NOTAMs and traffic need signal)

Live at https://meee1.github.io/airspace_assist/app/ (GitHub Pages, from `master`, repo `meee1/airspace_assist`).

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **The AIP always comes first.** ERSA and the DAH are the primary source for every frequency, aerodrome and airspace limit. Other data is only a fallback where the AIP has nothing, and must be tagged with its source in the UI:
   - `VATPAC`: the vatSys dataset, which is a flight-sim dataset
   - `OurAirports`
   - `XC Australia`: unofficial, and only used if no DAH is loaded
2. **Never invent or "fix" a frequency, limit or procedure.**
   - If the AIP is ambiguous, show what it says and point the pilot to the ATIS, ERSA or NOTAM. Moorabbin's two tower frequencies are the example: they are labelled East/West only because ERSA's procedure text ties each to a side.
   - When sources disagree, show the AIP value and mention the other source as a note ("OurAirports says …").
3. **Safety wording stays.** Keep these in place and don't weaken them:
   - "Not an authoritative source"
   - the effective-date banner
   - the note that GPS altitude is geometric, not pressure altitude
   - the note that terrain is for awareness only
4. Copy is plain and direct, aimed at a pilot reading a phone in flight.

## Build (each AIRAC)

```
# inputs: data/aip/<ERSA>.pdf, data/aip/DAH_<date>.pdf, optional XC file,
# plus vatSys + OurAirports files in a data dir (see README "Refresh data each AIRAC")
cd tools
python3 build_data.py <datadir> data.json --ersa <ERSA.pdf|txt> --dah <DAH.pdf> --xc "<openair.txt>"
python3 - <<'P'   # add WMM variation grid (see README for the exact snippet)
...
P
python3 assemble.py && cp dist/* ../app/
```
- Needs `pip install pygeomag pillow pymupdf` and `pdftotext` (poppler).
- **UI-only change:** edit `tools/template.html`, re-run `assemble.py` against the existing `data.json`, then copy `dist/*` to `app/`. Don't edit `app/index.html` or `app/app.js` directly.
- `data.json` keys:
  - `ap`: aerodromes.
    - Source codes `cS`/`aS`/`wS`/`xS` are `E`=ERSA, `X`=XC, `V`=VATPAC, `O`=OurAirports. Conflicts are in `cf`.
    - ERSA extras: `st`/`utc` (state, UTC offset), `cnotes` (COMM notes incl. TWR HR), `proc` (procedure text), `keys` (CTR entry, circuit alt, reporting points, heli alt), `cct` (RH/LH circuits by runway end, with ERSA wording), `var`.
    - `x` tower entries may carry `[…, 'East'|'West', evidence]` from ERSA procedure text.
    - `rw`: `[end1, end2, lat1, lon1, lat2, lon2, lenM, surface, widthM, src, schematic]`. `src` is `DAH` (thresholds) or `ERSA`; schematic 1 = ERSA bearing, 2 = designator heading.
    - `t: 'H'` marks hospital/heliport sites by name; they're hidden unless "Show heliports" is on.
  - `asp`: airspace, as `[cls, name, lo, loKind, hi, hiKind, freq, poly, extra]`. Classes include `BA` (broadcast area), `FIA`, `EF` (class E frequency). `extra.u` lists ERSA controlling units; `extra.cc` is the CTR class.
  - `vw`: VFR waypoints `[code, name, lat, lon]` (ERSA).
  - `na`: navaids `[ident, type, name, lat, lon, freq]` (DAH + ERSA frequency).
  - `iw`: IFR waypoints `[name, lat, lon]` (DAH). Used for search/simulation only.
  - `sec`/`bnd`: vatSys sectors.
  - `meta`: ERSA/DAH dates.
  - `var`: WMM variation grid.
- Browser storage keys are all prefixed `rc_`:
  - `fp`: flight plan (`rev` counts edits), `fpAddAt` (where the Edit search adds: end / best / next)
  - `goto`
  - `mapRange`, `mapUp`, `mapCtaf`, `mapLead` (lead line, default on), `mapLog` (flight log on the map, default off), `mapTer` (terrain shading on the map, default on), `mapCharts`, `chartOp`
  - `sim`, `simOn`, `simOpen`
  - `effOpen_<date>`
  - `cType`, `cSign`, `cCat`, `cMode`
  - `ahead`, `heli`, `all`, `theme`, `started`, `wake` (keep screen on)
  - `cmp`: compass alignment `{off, n, s, c}`
  - `notamUrl` (the traffic proxy address; unset = `NT_DEFAULT` https://test.mission.workers.dev, empty string = off), `notam` (old proxy NOTAMs, cleared at start-up), `mapTraffic`, `trVoice`
  - `tlog`: flight log (GPS fixes every 5 s for 6 h, plus waypoint-change events)

## Gotchas

- `fetch_srtm.py` arguments with negative numbers need `=`: `--around=-38.15,144.36,300`, `--bbox=-39.5,140.5,-33.5,150.5`.
- Line endings: `fetch_srtm.bat`, the XC `.txt` and `data/abs/UCL_2021_AUST_GDA2020.xml` show CRLF-only diffs from Linux/WSL shells. Don't commit those as changes; `git add` the files you changed by name.
- **Commit and push only when the owner says so.** Commit identity: `git -c user.name="Michael Oborne" -c user.email="mich146@hotmail.com" commit …` (the shell has none set), ending the message with the Co-Authored-By line. Push: the owner keeps a fine-grained PAT in the claude.ai project doc "GitHub pat". Read it with the Projects tool and push from the owner's computer shell with `git -c http.extraHeader="Authorization: Basic $(printf 'x-access-token:%s' "$T" | base64 -w0)" push origin master`, filtering the token out of any output. Never write it to git config, a file or a commit. The cloud session's own GitHub token has no access to this repo. The owner can also push from Windows (cmd, TortoiseGit). Check what's live with `git ls-remote origin master` and the `radio-au-<hash>` cache name in the served `sw.js`.
- Copy the built files into place *before* writing them to the repo, never in parallel with that write: commit acd34a4 shipped a correct app.js but a stale tools/template.html because the copy and the device write raced. Check `md5sum` of tools/template.html and app/*.js against the build before committing.
- After copying files into the repo from another environment, compare checksums (`md5sum`) with the build. A copy has silently left a stale `app/index.html` once, and the live site shipped without the change. `app/index.html`, `app/app.js`, `app/data.json`, `app/sw.js` and `tools/template.html` must come from the same build. The loader fetches `data.json?v=<hash>` and `app.js?v=<hash>`, so a mismatch shows up as stale data, not an error.
- Airservices, xcaustralia.org and the SRTM hosts may be unreachable from sandboxed shells. The owner downloads the PDFs and runs `fetch_srtm.bat` locally.
- `data/aip` holds Airservices copyright material (ERSA, DAH, VTC/VNC) in a public repo. Consider `.gitignore`-ing `data/`, since only the generated app is needed for Pages. Ask the owner before changing this.
- The app carries two AIRAC sets (see Two AIRAC data sets below). It shows a banner when the data isn't effective yet and warns when it's more than two cycles old.
- Tower-hours logic (`twrStatus`) parses ERSA "TWR HR" / "TWR and APP HR" notes, using local times and HDS by state. It handles several ranges per line (Jandakot, Richmond), a "During HDS:" sub-list (Moorabbin), "(1HR earlier during HDS)" on the line or as a block note (Bankstown, Coffs: the UTC times move an hour, the local clock stays), seasonal JUN-AUG/SEP-MAY qualifiers, and skips "O/R"/"PN" on-request ranges. Unknown hours → the tower is assumed active (it used to assume closed when a CTAF was published, which showed Canberra and Hobart as CTAF all day). DST boundaries are Saturday 1600 UTC (first Sunday Oct/Apr 0200/0300 local). Test: `play/twr.js` has 28 dated cases. It doesn't know about public holidays or NOTAMs, and the UI says so.
- Outside tower hours, a Class D zone is shown as class G only where ERSA says it in one plain sentence ("Outside TWR HR airspace becomes Class G", `REV` in template.html). Avalon (becomes E) and zones whose wording has altitude limits or CTA conditions (Albury, Alice Springs, Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Broome, Tamworth, Hobart, Launceston, Canberra) stay as published. Don't widen this without reading each ERSA entry.
- ERSA entries without `ELEV` on the name line (water aerodromes, offshore helidecks) are found from the state/UTC/ICAO line. Without that, their COMM lines merge into the entry before. The ICAO look-ahead after ELEV is 12 lines and the ARP search runs to the COMM block (diagram labels sit between): with 5/14 lines YCAB, YGDA, YGBI and YWSG were dropped and Busselton inherited Caboolture's procedures. A section heading (LOCAL TRAFFIC REGULATIONS etc.) must start in column 0; an indented caps line like Jandakot's `RWY HJ HN` table header is content. AFIS is a service type. The state regex has no leading `\b` (`19NSW`).
- With a flight plan loaded, the aerodrome card follows the plan (`fpAerodrome`): the next plan aerodrome within 10 NM; that aerodrome within 15 NM when the active waypoint is one of its ERSA reporting points; then the last plan aerodrome within 5 NM; otherwise the closest. A pin still wins.
- The flight plan elevation profile (`profBuild`/`drawProf`) samples the plan about every 0.2 NM (500 samples max). It calls `terrainFt` and `airStack` at each sample and caches the result on a key: plan, tiles loaded, minute. Tile loads trigger `render()` and so a rebuild. Keep `airStack` cheap, because the profile multiplies its cost by about 500.
- The CTAF call location (`callName`) is normally the part after `/` in the ERSA name: MELBOURNE/MOORABBIN gives "Moorabbin", and BROOME/INTL falls back to "Broome". Where local usage differs, `CALL_AS` overrides it, with the source in a comment. The owner confirmed YBRS (BARWON HEADS/GEELONG) is called "Barwon Heads traffic". Add entries only from someone who flies there, not by guessing. Other slash names that may need checking: Kingscote/Kangaroo Island, Peterborough/Great Ocean Road, Ballina/Byron Gateway, Proserpine/Whitsunday Coast, Ayers Rock/Connellan.
- Airservices sells airspace as shapefiles (Shape Files, $5,950/yr) and CSV (Airspace Data, $1,950/yr) from data.airservicesaustralia.com, with no free download. The owner chose to stay on the free DAH PDF (Oct 2026). Don't add a shapefile path unless the owner provides the files.
- Offline: `assemble.py` writes the service worker. It precaches all terrain tiles after activation into `radio-au-terrain` (4 at a time), which survives app updates. Build files (app.js, data.json, data-next.json, urban.json) are cache-first from the per-build cache `radio-au-<hash>`, so code and data always come from the same build; the page and everything else is network-first with a 3 s timeout, then that cache. Chart tiles and `lib/` live in `radio-au-charts`, fonts in `radio-au-fonts`. The footer `#offl` shows what's stored. Test with Playwright `context.setOffline(true)` after the SW is ready. The SW registers on https or localhost only.
- **App build time** (`APP_BUILT`): `assemble.py` replaces `/*__BUILT__*/0` with the build time in ms, and `setFoot` shows "App updated <local date time> (<age>)" at the top of the footer, so you can tell which version a phone is running. Every build changes it (and so the SW cache name), even with no code change.
- Traffic (TCAS-style): built 28 Oct 2026 through the proxy (`/traffic` in tools/notam-worker/worker.js, adsb.lol then adsb.fi, free, no key; airplanes.live now needs their approval (403 "please contact us"); `ADSB_URL`/`ADSB_KEY` for adsb.lol or adsb.fi). Before that it was shelved because api.adsb.lol sends no CORS header and allows 500 requests a day per IP, and Wi-Fi ADS-B boxes can't be reached from https. A USB 1090 receiver over WebUSB is still the way to get traffic without internet.
- TAF decoder: abandoned (Oct 2026). The BOM TAF lookup (POST https://www.bom.gov.au/aviation/php/process.php, keyword=<ICAO>&type=search&page=TAF, returns HTML with TAF + METAR) sends no CORS headers, so it can't be fetched from meee1.github.io. aviationweather.gov was also blocked from the page. The options were a paste box with a BOM link, or a proxy (e.g. a Cloudflare Worker) hosted by the owner.

## Feature notes

- **GPS altitude datum** (`GEOID`, `GEO`, `geoidN`, `GPS_ELLIPSOID`, in `onPos`): the W3C Geolocation API and Android's `Location.getAltitude()` give height above the WGS84 ellipsoid; SRTM heights and AIP limits are above the EGM96 geoid. `onPos` subtracts N = `geoidN(lat,lon)` (bilinear on the 0.25° nodes of `tools/egm96_au.json`, int16 cm, base64, 153×265 nodes, ~108 KB in app.js) unless the UA is iOS (Core Location's `altitude` is already MSL). Outside the grid N is null and nothing is subtracted. `st.geoidN` keeps the value; the ALT tile's title shows it. Checked against the egm96-universal reference to 0.1 m at YBRS +2.0, YMMB +4.2, Sydney +22.2, Cairns +62.2, Perth −31.7, Darwin +50.9 (`play` test feeds a Cairns fix of 162.2 m ellipsoid and gets 328 ft). Simulation altitudes are taken as MSL and not corrected. Before 28 Oct 2026 there was no correction.
- **NOTAMs** (`NTN`, `ntBox`, `ntFor`, `ntAt`, `ntForArea`, `ntRoute`): from a NAIPS briefing only. The FAA NMS feed through the proxy (Michael's "live via a proxy", 28 Oct) was removed on 29 Oct 2026 at his request (`NT`/`ntRefresh`/`/notams` gone; NMS credentials had never worked: token 401). `ntActive` uses `from`/`to` (`PERM` = never ends) and the NAIPS active periods.
  - **Display:** tile suffix `ntCountTxt`; `#adNotam` (opens itself for CLSD/U/S/NOT AVBL); `#fpNotam` (`ntRoute`: plan aerodromes, plus circles within 1 NM of a leg sampled every 2 NM); map menu (`ntAt`: circle contains the point); SUA blocks (`ntForArea`: the ident in the text); map circles for Q codes other than aerodrome/facility ones (`^Q(M|F|L|C|N|I|P|S|A)` excluded), r 0.3–60 NM. Times: `ntWhen` prints UTC `DD Mon HHMMZ` with local time in brackets.
  - **NAIPS paste** (`naipsParse`, `NTN` = `rc_naips`, `ntAllItems` = the briefing's items; `nbLoad`; manifest `share_target` POST `./share` multipart (title/text/file): sw.js parks it in cache `radio-au-share` (`share/file` with `x-name`, or `share/text`) and redirects to `./?shared=1`, which the app reads and clears; the old GET `?text=` is still accepted; **PDF**: `pdfText` rebuilds the fixed-width lines from pdf.js item positions (median char width, left margin) so the indentation rules still work, dropping browser print headers/footers; `nbFile`; **Brief in NAIPS** posts the Briefing/Area form (BriefingArea1-5 from `rc_nbAreas`, Notam only, Validity 12) to a new tab: the app never fetches NAIPS itself): parses the NAIPS text layout: `hhmm UTC dd/mm/yy` header (year and month for the `FROM MM DDHHMM TO MM DDHHMM [EST]|PERM` lines; a month more than 6 away rolls the year), `VALID FROM … TO hhmm UTC MON dd, yyyy`, column-0 headers `NAME (CODE)` (CODE may be YMMM/YBBB, R352, ESX), indented `C123/26 [REPLACE C120/26]` ids, body lines until FROM, then schedule lines and `yymmddhhmm TO yymmddhhmm` periods (`n.per`, used by `ntActive`), and the trailing "no current NOTAM" list (`NTN.none`). Limits `SFC|nnFT AMSL|AGL|FLnnn TO …` (`agl` flag), circles `WI n NM|M|KM RADIUS OF ARP|PSN ddmmss[.s]S dddmmss[.s]E`, else the first PSN, else the aerodrome. Tested on excerpts of a real Area 9300 briefing (28 Oct 2026) covering each pattern above; not yet run on a whole briefing in the app.
  - **NAIPS login (owner's request, not built yet):** the app was to send a NAIPS username/password to the proxy, which logs in (POST /naips/Account/LogOn, fields UserName/Password, no captcha) and requests a briefing. Blocked on the briefing request form, which is behind the login; Claude can't sign in with a password, so the owner needs to sign in in the browser pane for the form to be read. Airservices' NIS FAQ says scripted log-in-and-pull "won't work" and machine formats need a formal agreement.
  - **Test:** `play/naips.js` (cards, map menu, R352/R360D) and `play/nbpdf.js` (PDF via file picker and share target, text share, Brief in NAIPS).
- **Traffic** (`TR`, `trPoll`, `trTargets`, `trAlert`, `drawTraffic`, `#mapTraffic` in the first map-control row, `#sTraffic` row; display = the owner's 27 Oct pick):
  - **Map:** every airborne target gets a 15 px track stick (`t.ux/uy`); tap-select via `MAP.trHits` (24 px, checked before waypoint picks; a miss clears `TR.sel`); the selected one gets a dashed ring and a details box drawn last (`MAP.trBox`, placed right/left/below/above clear of the target, its stick and ownship). Test: `play/trsel.js`.
  - `trPoll` every 5 s when `MAP.traffic`, a proxy URL, a position, online and the page visible: `/traffic?lat&lon&nm=25`.
