Uses your GPS position (and compass when stationary) to find the closest aerodrome and the area frequency for where you are.
Decimal degrees, DMS, an aerodrome code, a VFR waypoint (CARR), a navaid (AV) or an IFR waypoint. Fly moves you along the track at the set ground speed. Tap an aerodrome in Nearby to select it.
    Imports the route (rtept) from a GPX file, e.g. from OzRunways or AvPlan; if there's no route it uses the waypoints (wpt), then a track (trkpt). Points whose names match an aerodrome, VFR waypoint or navaid are labelled from the AIP. The next waypoint advances automatically within 0.5 NM or once you pass it, and drives Go to. The profile shows SRTM terrain and DAH airspace along the straight legs: tap it for details. It is for awareness, not obstacle clearance. In simulation, Fly follows the route.
