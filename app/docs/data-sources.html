    Data sources and limits

    Where everything in the app comes from, how sources are tagged, and what the app doesn't know.

    Not an authoritative source. Confirm against the current ERSA, charts, NOTAMs and ATIS.

    The AIP comes first

    • ERSA and the DAH are the primary source for every frequency, aerodrome and airspace limit.
    • Other data is used only where the AIP has nothing, and is tagged with its source. Every value carries a tag: green for AIP (ERSA, DAH, an AIP rule, a charted CTAF), orange for anything else.
    • Where sources disagree, the AIP value is shown and the other mentioned as a note ("OurAirports says …").
    • The app never makes up or "fixes" a frequency, limit or procedure. Where the AIP is unclear (Moorabbin's two tower frequencies, for example) it shows what the AIP says and points you to the ATIS.

    Sources

    Source (tag)Used for
    ERSA (Airservices)Aerodromes, positions, elevations; CTAF, ATIS, AWIS, tower, ground, delivery, approach, departures, UNICOM; area frequency per aerodrome; tower hours; procedures and reporting points; runways (lengths, surface, width); right-hand circuits; VFR waypoints; navaid frequencies; the SUA list (restricted/danger area status, hours, activity, contacts); FAC sheets; the controlling unit for each zone and step.
    DAH (Airservices)All airspace (C, D, E, A, control zones, restricted, danger, military operating areas) with step limits; area (FIA) and class E frequency boundaries; broadcast areas; runway thresholds; navaids; IFR waypoints (search and simulation only). Boundaries that follow a coast, river or road are drawn as straight lines between the published points.
    VTC / VNC (charted CTAF)Where ERSA has no CTAF but the chart prints one beside the aerodrome, that frequency (AIP ENR 1.1 makes it the CTAF), tagged VTC, VNC or VTC/VNC with the sheet named.
    AIP defaultAny other aerodrome: CTAF 126.7, or the broadcast area frequency inside one.
    VATPACThe vatSys flight-sim dataset. Only names a controlling unit where ERSA has none (e.g. class A).
    OurAirportsStrips not in ERSA (with Include non-ERSA strips on), and notes where it disagrees.
    XC AustraliaUnofficial airspace, only if no DAH is loaded.

    AIRAC sets and the effective-date banner

    • The app carries the AIRAC data in force when it was built and, near a change, the next one. It picks whichever is in force on the phone's clock: AIRAC data takes effect at 1600 UTC the day before the effective date (AIP GEN 3.1).
    • Both sets are stored for offline use. If the change happens while the app is open, a banner offers a reload.
    • The effective-date banner shows when the data isn't in force yet, and the app warns when the data is more than two cycles old.
    • The ERSA FAC sheets are from the same cycle as the frequencies on screen.

    What the app doesn't know

    • Public holidays and NOTAM changes to tower hours. Confirm on the ATIS.
    • NOTAMs you haven't loaded. There is no live feed; get a fresh NAIPS briefing before each flight.
    • Pressure altitude. GPS altitude is geometric, not pressure altitude. Near a step, go by the altimeter.
    • Obstacles other than NOTAM obstacles: masts, buildings and most wires aren't in the terrain. Terrain is for awareness only.
    • Traffic not transmitting ADS-B or not heard by a ground receiver. It isn't TCAS.
    • Winds aloft: the NAIPS text briefing has none; enter the grid point wind yourself.
    • Aircraft performance: W&B and hover figures are only what you enter from your flight manual.
    • What was said on the radio: transcription mishears. It's an aid only.
    • Local procedures beyond ERSA: check the clearance request and call wording against AIP ENR 1.1 and local procedures.
