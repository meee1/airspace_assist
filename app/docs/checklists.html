    Checklists and passenger briefing

    Checklists live under Flight plan → Checklists. Tick items by tap or by voice, have the right list come up by itself at each phase of flight, and show a passenger briefing full screen.

    Checklist items come from your flight manual and operator's procedures. The app doesn't invent any.

    Built-in Bell 206B3 checklist

    The app comes with a Bell 206B3 checklist transcribed as written from an operator's sheet, in four lists:

    • Pre start
    • Start (with "Start – continued")
    • Pre lift off checks
    • Shutdown (offered after landing)

    You can edit or delete them. A deleted one stays deleted, also across your devices. Check them against your own operator's checklist before use.

    Making and editing a checklist

    • Add your own by typing or pasting from the flight manual, one item per line.
    • A line starting with # is a heading.
    • Item .... response (or Item : response) puts the response on the right.
    • Choose the aircraft (your weight and balance profiles) or every aircraft.
    • Offer it chooses when it comes up by itself (below), or Passenger briefing.
    • Lists combine across your devices through Google Drive and travel in the settings backup and file.

    Running a checklist

    • Open one and tap items to tick them. The next item is marked and the count shows at the top.
    • Reset clears the ticks.
    • Ticks stay on this device and survive closing the app.

    Offered by phase of flight

    Offer itComes up when
    Before take-offYou start moving (5 kt or more) after being stopped for a minute.
    Pre-landingThe 10 NM inbound call fires for an aerodrome you're landing at.
    After landingYou've stopped for 30 s after flying at 30 kt or more.

    The list's ticks are cleared and a CHECKLIST row shows ("Pre-landing · Before landing · tap to open") until it's complete, or for 10 minutes. Tap the row to open it. With spoken alerts on, its name is said aloud.

    Hands-free

    In a checklist, 🎙 Hands-free reads the next item aloud and listens for your response through the input picked under Radio audio and transcription. With the Whisper engine, download the speech model first.

    SayDoes
    check, checked, done, next, set, on, off, complete, confirmed… or the item's own responseTicks it and reads the next.
    repeat, say againReads it again.
    back, previousUnticks the one before.
    stop, cancel, pause, finishEnds hands-free.
    • Section headings are read when they change. At the end it says "<name> complete" and stops.
    • What the microphone hears while the tablet is speaking is ignored, so its own voice can't tick an item.
    • A tap on an item still works. Closing the checklist stops it.

    Passenger briefing card

    • Make a checklist with Offer it: Passenger briefing and type your briefing items (from your operator's procedures or flight manual; the app has none of its own).
    • More → Passenger brief shows them full screen, one at a time in large text with the section heading, for passengers to read with you. Tap the text or Next to go on; Back; ✕ or Esc closes.
    • Done ticks the list and records the time.
    • With several briefing lists for the aircraft, it shows the list to pick from. With none yet, the button starts one. A briefing list's run view also has Full screen.
